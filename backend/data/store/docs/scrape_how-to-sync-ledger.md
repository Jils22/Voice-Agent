# How to Sync Ledger

Sync Ledger keeps your Tally party names updated in Suvit, ensuring accurate and consistent ledger data for smooth integration and error-free transactions.

### **Steps to Sync Ledger:**

#### **Step 1 - Open Suvit.io in Browser**

1. Log in to your **Suvit.io** account.  
   ![1dashboard.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1dashboard_6407fc8358.png)

#### **Step 2 - Open Tally and Select the Company**

![2image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2image_f215b997f2.png)

* Launch **Tally** on your system.
* Select the company you want to sync.

#### **Step 3 - Use Suvit Desktop Application**

![3sync_master.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3sync_master_a50e0161c9.png)

* Open the **Suvit Desktop Application**.
* Navigate to **My Companies** and find the company where **"Sync Master"** is available.
* Click on **"Sync Master"**.

#### **Step 4 - Verify Successful Sync**

![4sync_master1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4sync_master1_f2e8585b93.png)

* Once you see a popup confirming the ledger synced successfully, go to **Suvit Web Login**.
* Refresh the page using **CTRL+SHIFT+R**.
* Navigate to the **Banking Module** and verify the bank name appears.
