# Create Invoice for Sales and Sales-Return

This guide helps you efficiently create Sales and Sales Return invoices in Suvit, ensuring accurate data entry and smooth integration with Tally.

### 🎥 Watch Video Guide

---

### 🪜 Step-by-Step Guide

---

#### ✅ Step 1: Go to Dashboard

![1.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_238d86c7be.webp)

* On the dashboard, click **"Explore Now"** under **Data Entry Automation**

---

#### ✅ Step 2: Create Invoice

![2.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_0e413daab0.webp)

* In the **Transaction Menu**, click **Create Bill**
* Choose whether this is a **Sales** or **Sales Return** invoice

---

#### ✅ Step 3: Choose Invoice Type

![3 invoice.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_invoice_dec448e451.png)

* Pick one of the two:

  → **Accounting Invoice** = Without Stock Items

  → **Item Invoice** = With Stock Items (use this if you’re selling goods)

---

#### ✅ Step 4: Fill in Invoice Details

![4 accunting invoice.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_accunting_invoice_18312122fb.png)

* Choose the following:

  → **Voucher Type** (e.g. Sales)

  → **Voucher No.** (like invoice number)

  → **Voucher Date** (date of invoice)

  → **Party Name** (customer name)

  → **GST Details** will auto-fill from Tally

  → **Ledger Name** (like "Sales @ 18%")

---

#### ✅ Step 5: Customer Details View

![5 cstomer.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/5_cstomer_7b548436c8.png)

* The customer details on the left will show here too
* Other values will sync from Tally automatically

---

##### How to access configuration - Use the **Configuration window** to select the fields displayed during voucher entry. - such as **Voucher Date**, **Supplier Invoice No.**, **Item Narration**, etc. Save the changes once done. 4_1_con_d05990b1df.png

---

#### ✅ Step 6: Add Items (For "Item Invoice" only)

![5 item.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/5_item_e03a24d7d7.png)

* Pick your **Stock Items** from the dropdown
* Enter the **Quantity** (e.g., 5, 10, 50)
* Enter the **Rate** (price per item)

```
💡 If you have multiple items, just keep adding them one by one!
```

---

#### ✅ Step 7: Taxes, Narration & Amounts

![8 SAVE IMG.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/8_SAVE_IMG_93d54e10f7.png)

* Select **Tax Ledgers** like SGST, CGST, IGST
* Add any **narration or comments** (optional)
* Double-check the **Taxable Amount** and **Total Amount**

---

#### ✅ Step 8: Save the Bill

* Click **Save & Close** to finish
* Click **Save & Sync** if you want to send it to Tally right away

```
📌 Fields marked with "*" must be filled in — no skipping!
```
