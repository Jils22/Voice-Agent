### Steps to Work with Banking Transactions in Suvit

Here are the steps to filter and work with transactions in the Suvit platform:

1. **Login to [Suvit](https://in.suvit.io/signIn):**

* Navigate to the **Data Entry Automation** option and click **Explore Now** to proceed.
  ![Dashboard](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/dash_3a1d11a7c4.png)

2. **Access Banking Section:**

* Go to the **Banking** section.
  ![Banking](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1bank_39d6c9430e.png)

3. **Select the Statement:**

* On the **Banking** page, click on **Complete** for the statement you want to work with.
  ![Complete](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2complete_1df21c050a.png)

4. **Filter Transactions by Date:**

* On the **Bank Transaction** page, use the **General Filters** option to select a specific **Date Range**.
  ![Date Filter Step 1](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3date_c73350f6d1.png)
  ![Date Filter Step 2](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4datee_9b9c1d81ee.png)
  ![Date Filter Step 3](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/5date1_2b16e44760.png)

5. **View Filtered Transactions:**

* After selecting the desired date range, you'll see the filtered list of transactions that you can work on.

By following these steps, you can efficiently manage and filter your bank transactions in Suvit for further processing.
