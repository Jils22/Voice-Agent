# How to buy Suvit Subscription

Suvit Subscription can be purchased directly through website. This article suggests plans, details & features that can fit to your use case.

---

### Here's a step-by-step guide to help you get started:

#### Step 1: Login

![1.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_b5a74e3caa.webp)

* Login into [Suvit](https://in.suvit.io/signIn)

**1** - Go To **My-Profile**

**2** - Click on **Subscription & Billing**

**3** - Click on **Buy Now**

#### Step 2: Make a Payment

![2.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_020296824c.webp)

* Before proceeding with the **Payment** complete **Billing Info**

**4** - Add **Pincode**

**5** - Click on **Save**

**6** - Click on **Make a Payment**

#### Step 3: Payment Page

![3.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_002e959761.webp)

**7** - Enter your **Registered Phone number & E-mail id** & click on **Continue**

#### Step 4: Payment Gateway

* Choose your desired **Mode of Payment**

**8** - Click on **Continue**

* Once your payment is complete, your subscription will **activate** immediately.(*Payment made through any link can take few hours to activate your account.*)
  ![4.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_49de7e64d2.webp)

### What Can You Do With Your Suvit Subscription?

Here’s what you can do once you have Suvit:

1. **Upload Unlimited Documents:** You can add all your important documents!
2. **Banking Module:** Process payments, receipts, and other transactions easily.
3. **Sales/Purchase Module:** Handle sales, purchases, and receipts like a pro.
4. **Bulk Master Creation:** Create many stock items and ledgers all at once.
5. **Client Management:** Give your clients a Suvit ID to upload their documents, and everything will show up in your account.
6. **Manage Your Team:** Assign tasks to your staff using Suvit and keep everything organized.
7. **Beta Program:** Have early access to upcoming features.
