# How to book Suvit training session by yourself?

Learn how to easily book Suvit training sessions from your dashboard. Select module, choose date & time, fill details, and get confirmation.

### How to Book Suvit Training Webinar by Yourself

#### Step 1: Go to Dashboard

→ Login to [Taxone Vyapar](https://taxone.vyapar.com/app/home/dashboard)

→ Click on **Dashboard**

→ You will see three training modules: **Banking, Sales/Purchase, GST Reconciliation.**

→ Select desired **module** & **time**
![1 (2).webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_2_3be799abf3.webp)

---

#### Step 2: Select Date

→ Under the training session of your choice, click on **Select Date**.

![2 (2).webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_2_46b013920e.webp)

---

#### Step 3: Choose Date

→ A list of available dates will appear.

→ Pick the slot that suits you best.

![3 (2).webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_2_5fcacc4881.webp)

---

#### Step 4: Fill Registration Form

→ Enter your **Registered**
\* **Full Name, Email, and Phone**.

→ Click on **Register**.

→ You’ll see a confirmation on screen & also receive an email.
![4 (2).webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_2_6175805543.webp)

---

✅ **Note: The joining link will be sent to your registered email**

### How can I book a 1:1 Sales/Purchase training session from the Suvit dashboard?

You can book a **1:1 training session only for the Sales/Purchase module** directly from your Suvit dashboard.

#### Step 1: Open the 1:1 booking for Sales/Purchase

* Go to the **Dashboard** in Suvit.
* In the **Sales/Purchase – Online Training Session** card, click on the **Book 1:1** button.
  → This button is only for **Sales/Purchase 1:1** sessions.

![self1.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/self1_658fb33107.webp)

#### Step 2: Select your preferred date & Time

* A popup titled **Online Training Session** will open.
* In the top section, choose your preferred **date** from the available options.
  → Use the left/right arrows to move between dates if needed.
* In the **Choose a time** section, select the **time slot** that suits you.
* Once selected, the chosen time will be highlighted/marked as selected.

![self2.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/self2_3b332b97ee.webp)

#### Step 3: Click on the “Book” button

* After selecting both **date** and **time**, click on the **Book** button at the bottom-right of the popup.
* This will confirm your selected slot for the **1:1 Sales/Purchase session.**

![self3.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/self3_4ec344e19f.webp)

#### Step 4: Check the booking confirmation

* **Booking Confirmed** popup will appear with:

  → Date of the session
  → Time of the session

![self4.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/self4_33c833f400.webp)

* Confirmation email with all details will also be sent to your registered email address.
