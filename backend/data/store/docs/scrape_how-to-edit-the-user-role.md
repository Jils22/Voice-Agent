# How to edit the user role?

After successfully creating a user role, given future business requirements, if you need to change it, refer this article to understand the steps involved.

---

### Steps to Edit the User Role:

#### Step 1: Sign In

![1 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_img_c65561eb63.png)

* [Sign in](https://in.suvit.io/dashboard) to Suvit with the Primary account (**Main Account**).

#### Step 2: Access Role Management

![1role5.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1role5_40e51adc70.png)

* Click on **Role Management** from the left menu.

#### Step 3: Edit Role

![2role6.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2role6_74345e15d1.png)

* On the Role Management page, you will see a list of roles you have created on the right-hand side. In the **Action** section, you will find the **edit role** option.

#### Step 4: Update Role

![3role7.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3role7_6ba286567d.png)

* After clicking the **edit role** button, you will be redirected to the edit role page. Make the necessary changes and press the **"Update role"** button.

#### Step 5: Confirm Role Update

* You will be redirected to the Roles page with a successful role update message.
