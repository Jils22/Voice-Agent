# How to Access and Review Secondary User Reports in Suvit

Suvit’s “Secondary User Reports” feature lets you monitor activities of secondary users easily. Track user actions and maintain control from a central dashboard.

---

### **"Secondary User Reports"**

#### Step 1: **Sign In to Suvit with the Primary Account**

![1 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_img_0ec33378d7.png)

* [Sign in](https://in.suvit.io/signIn) to Suvit using your **Primary Account** (**Main Account**).

#### Step 2: **Access the User Report Tab**

![2image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2image_f26a0f1e74.png)

* Click on **Users** from the left menu.
* Within the **"Users"** section, you will find a tab labeled **"User Report"**. This tab is where you can access and review the secondary user reports. Click on the **"User Report"**.

#### Step 3: **Explore Module-Wise Secondary User Reports**

* Upon clicking the **"User Report"** tab, you will be directed to a page where you can explore secondary user reports in a module-wise fashion. The secondary user reports will provide a detailed breakdown of the activities performed by the secondary users. Here, you can view data related to transactions, uploads, saves, and synchronization with Tally.

"Secondary User Reports"

In conclusion, Suvit's feature offers a powerful tool for tracking and monitoring the activities of secondary users. By following these simple steps, you can easily access and review these reports, gaining a comprehensive understanding of their contributions and interactions with the platform. This, in turn, helps to enhance efficiency, accountability, and data management practices within your Suvit workspace.
