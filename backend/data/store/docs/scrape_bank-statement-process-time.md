# Bank statement processing time

Once you upload a bank statement, it takes some time to get processed, depending on the complexity, type and format of the statement.

---

### Bank Statement Processing Times

Bank statement processing time depends on the number of entries and the structure of the document. In the case of scanned documents, it also depends on the clarity of the document.

### Standard Processing Times:

Click to view bank statement processing time

* **Excel**: Up-to 30 min
* **Pdf**: Up-to 1 hour
* **Scan**: Up-to 12 hours

```
Suvit does not support consolidated documents, documents which are not merged in sequence, 
landscape document, Handwritten content on bank statement, single pdf with multiple accounts, 
multiple columns of credit & debit entries.
```
