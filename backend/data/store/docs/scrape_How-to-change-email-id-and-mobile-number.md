# How to change email id and mobile number

To update your email or mobile number in Suvit, follow the steps provided in this guide for a quick and easy update to your account details.

#### Step 1: My-Profile

![1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_351c11a52f.png)

* Log in to Suvit and navigate to the **"My Profile"** section.
* Click on **Change Email** or **Change Mobile**

#### Step 2: Enter Password

![2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_5fc16dab85.png)

* To change your account details enter your current password.
* Click on **Verify Password**

#### Step 3: Change Details

![3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_a422261a1b.png)

* Enter your new **E-mail id**
* Click on **Verify**

#### Step 4: Save

![4.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_ea04a05254.png)

* Enter **OTP** received on **new E-mail id**
* Click on **Verify**
* Upon successful verification, your email address will be updated.
