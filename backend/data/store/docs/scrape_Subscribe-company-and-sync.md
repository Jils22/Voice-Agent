# Subscribe to the company and synchronize the ledgers

To process your data in tally, company needs to be subscribed in Suvit and its ledgers should be synced for selecting it to transactions.

Suvit helps you automate transferring your various data into Tally, such as:

* [Bank Statement](https://help.suvit.io/collections/banking)
* [Sales and Sales Return](https://help.suvit.io/collections/sales-and-sales-return)
* [Purchase and Purchase Return](https://help.suvit.io/collections/purchase-and-purchase-return)
* [Journal Entries](https://help.suvit.io/collections/purchase-and-purchase-return)
* [Bulk Party/Stock Item Creation](https://help.suvit.io/collections/bulk-master-creation)
* [Client and User Management](https://help.suvit.io/collections/client-and-user-management)

##### To start using Suvit with your Tally company, follow these steps to subscribe and synchronize the data.

#### Step 1: Open the Company in Tally

![Open Company in Tally](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1image_18_af1af90563.png)

* **Open the company** you want to work with in Tally and make sure it is selected, as shown in the image above.

#### Step 2: Add company

![Suvit Connector](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_App_e06b00b59f.png)

* Open **Suvit Desktop Application** (you can find the application on desktop)
* Go to **"Add Companies"** in your Suvit Desktop Application.

#### Step 3: Subscribe company

![Company Syncing](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_App_sync_5e64443b5e.png)

* Click on **Subscribe** Button to add.

#### Step 4: Wait for Synchronization

![Sync Complete](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_App3_a207ca2957.png)

* Once you subscribe to the company, it will appear under **"My Companies"** as shown above.
* The sync will start automatically for the first time. Wait a few moments for the synchronization to get complete.

#### Step 5: Confirmation of Sync

![5 compleeted.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/5_compleeted_aad29e55b0.png)

* After the sync is done, you’ll see a **confirmation message** like in the image above. This means your Tally ledgers are now synced with Suvit, and you can use them while processing data.
* Now, you can begin uploading your documents in the desired modules and process the data in Tally.
