# Create bulk Stock Items in Suvit

Suvit lets you create stock items in bulk. Just prepare your Excel sheet as required and use the "Item" option under Bulk Upload to proceed easily.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Upload Stock Items in Bulk

#### Step 1: **Bulk Upload**

![2image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2image_d772a76c0c.png)

* Go to **Data Entry Automation** → **Bulk Upload** → **Item.**

#### Step 2: **Download the Sample Excel Sheet**

![3image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3image_85ced92794.png)

* On the **Item Excel page**, you will find an option to download a sample sheet or upload an existing Excel file.
* Click on **"Download Sample"** to get the template.

#### Step 3 **Fill in the Required Details**

![4image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4image_8462eb76f1.png)

* Open the excel sheet, fill in the required details, and save it. "**Name**", "**Under**", and "**Unit**" are mandatory fields to be filled in.

#### Step 4: **Upload the Excel Sheet**

![5image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/5image_93eb6d511a.png)

* Go back to the **Item Excel page**, click on "**Upload**," select the appropriate company, and browse your ledger Excel file for uploading.
* If any ledger fails to **upload**, you will see an error log showing the reason for the failure.

![6image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/6image_bf2a1d79ca.png)

* Click on the file for Next step.

#### Step 6: **Map the Fields**

![7image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/7image_95f3a2270c.png)

* Once uploaded, you will be redirected to the **Mapping Field** page. Here, you need to map the relevant data fields correctly.
* After completing the mapping, click on **Save & Proceed**, you will get an error popup if there's a mistake in the sheet. You can see the error in the popup or **Download excel error ledger log**.

#### Step 7: **Check for Remarks**

![8image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/8image_101e8fa4ce.png)

* On the **item transaction screen**, you will get the remark **"failed reason"**.

#### Step 8: **Save the Transaction**

![10image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/10image_18c0d3c01b.png)

* Go to the **Ledger Transaction** page, select the record, click **Save**, then click **"Send to Tally"** in the drawer's right corner.

#### Step 9: **Send the Transaction to Tally**

![12image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/12image_3069065318.png)

* A confirmation popup will appear → click **OK** to proceed or **Cancel** to stop.

![13image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/13image_a34d447377.png)

* The **data synchronization** with Tally is complete, confirming **successful syncing**.

#### Step 10: **Verify in Tally**

* Check those item ledgers in Tally. You will find that the stock item masters are created in your Tally.
