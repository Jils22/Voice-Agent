# Upload Invoice from Client Accounts

OCR converts scanned documents, PDFs, and images into searchable data. Easily upload sales and purchase files in Transactions for smooth processing in Suvit.

### How to Upload a "**Sales/Purchase**" Invoice?

* This Transactions section enables you to upload and manage various entries, such as **Sales & Purchases**.

##### Follow the steps below to upload an invoice easily:

#### Step 1: Access the Upload Option

![Transaction Upload Screen](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/transation_1_4c3b85e318.png)

* Navigate to the **Transactions** section from the left side.
* Click on **"Upload Image"** to start the process.

#### Step 2: Select the Invoice File & Upload

![Select File Screen](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/transation_2_aaddb18bfa.png)

* **Click on "Upload Image/PDF"** and **Select** the invoice from your system.
* Supported formats include **Image Files** and **PDF Documents**.
* Once the file is selected, click **"Upload"** to proceed.

#### Step 3: Review Uploaded Data

![Review Uploaded Data](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/transation_3_69eb9f3373.png)

* **View** the uploaded invoice details on the screen.
* **Verify** the extracted data for accuracy.
