# Company is showing disconnected

Suvit must stay connected with Tally and the Tally company. If a split or connectivity issue shows it as disconnected, follow this guide to resolve it.

### Overview

![1 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_img_8c06d668b4.png)  
This image provides an overview of the three steps required to connect Suvit with Tally using the Tally Connector Application. Follow each step carefully to ensure a successful connection.

### Follow three easy steps

#### Step 1: Open Tally and select your company

![2 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_img_bc94f21389.png)

* Ensure that **Tally is open** and the **company is selected in Tally.**

#### Step 2: Check and update Tally port (if needed)

![3 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_img_94af8b62e9.png)

* If the company still appears as disconnected in Suvit, verify and update the Tally port settings.
* To check or change the Tally port, refer to the guide: [Port Settings](https://help.suvit.io/articles/how-to-check-tally-port-or-change-port-in-tally-prime).

#### Step 3: Configure Suvit Desktop Tally Connector Application

![4 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_img_116769ac56.png)

* Open the Suvit Desktop Tally Connector Application on your system.
* Follow the step-by-step instructions to configure the Tally port within the connector.
* Detailed configuration guide: [Suvit Port Settings](https://help.suvit.io/articles/how-to-configure-tally-port-with-suvit).

✍ Once all three steps are completed, your Suvit application will successfully connect to Tally.
