# I am unable to see the company or ledger while uploading the document

If at the time of uploading the document you are unable to see/select company or ledger, it can be due to sync issue, read on for more.

---

* Usually, this happens if you have newly subscribed to a company and sync has not been done.
* Take this into account: before uploading a document, make sure the company opened in Tally is synced with Suvit.
* If you are a secondary user, ensure the company is assigned to you. [Learn more](https://help.suvit.io/articles/how-to-create-a-user-account-in-suvit#step-6-assign-company)

```
**Note:**
Whenever you create or alter a new ledger/item in Tally, simply "Sync Master" from the Suvit Tally Connector to get the latest data in Suvit.
```

### Steps to Sync the Company in Suvit:

#### Step 1. Open Suvit Application

![1 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_img_34ed0c8bec.png)

* Go to **"My Companies"** and click on the **"Sync Master"** button.

#### Step 2. Ledger Sync Successfully

![2 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_img_bee0913de8.png)

* Wait for the notification showing **"Ledger Sync Successfully."**

#### Step 3. Refresh Website

![3 img refresh.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_img_refresh_00c7de868e.png)

* Go to the website.
* **Refresh** the page **or** Press **CTRL + SHIFT + R** to hard refresh the page.
* Now, try to import/upload the document.

##### **If the issue still persists:** Kindly connect support team.
