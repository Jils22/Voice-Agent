# How to upload cash entry file in Banking

You can now upload a cash sheet in Suvit to process cash receipt and payment entries. A sample Excel file is attached in the article for your reference.

---

* **Bank Statement Sample Excel Sheet** can be used for regular **Bank Statement**, **Cash Entries**, **Loan Statement**, **Credit Card**.

### Follow the Steps

#### Download Sample Sheet

* Click here [Banking.xlsx](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Banking_25368c5355.xlsx) to download the sample sheet. **or**

![1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_3f7f3ca819.png)

* Go to → **Explore Now** under Data Entry Automation
* **Bulk Upload** → **Banking** → **Download Sample** file

#### Sample Excel Sheet

![cash _3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/cash_102031_a40aaa0f00.png)

* **Copy and Paste** your required data.
* **Date**, **Debit** & **Credit Amount** are three minimum required fields.
* Use **DD-MM-YYYY** for **Date format** & **Number Format** for Amounts.

#### Step 1: Import

![2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_bd415c877f.png)

* Click on **Import Button** to import

#### Step 2: Select File

![3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_3812a796ff.png)

* Click on the **SEE ALL** option to see all ledgers and select **"Cash"** Ledger and then **upload** your **Cash Excel sheet**.

**OR**

* Select your **Bank** and **Upload** the document
