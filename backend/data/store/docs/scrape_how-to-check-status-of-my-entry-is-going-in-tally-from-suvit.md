# How to check status of my entry is going in Tally from Suvit

Here we will guide you to identify the status of your entry that is been processed in tally from Suvit.

---

## How to Check the Status of My Entry in Tally from Suvit

Whenever anyone is sending data to Tally, a **Transaction Progress** screen will appear.

Here we have explained each stage of data push as per the image below:

![1Progress screen.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_Progress_screen_e8b0f98a09.jpg)

### Stages of Data Push:

1. **GREY color**: It shows that the entry has been initiated, also known as the 1st Stage.
2. **Orange color**: It shows that the entry has started, and data is being pushed to Tally. This is the 2nd Stage.
3. **GREEN color**: It shows that the data has been transferred successfully to Tally.
4. **RED color**: It shows that the entry has failed (data was not transferred to Tally).
5. **Click Here**: It will help you to identify the Failed reason.
