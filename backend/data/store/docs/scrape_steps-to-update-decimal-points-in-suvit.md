# How Many Decimal Points Does Suvit Support?

Suvit is designed to sync seamlessly with Tally. To ensure accurate data transfer and compatibility, Suvit adopts the decimal point settings configured in Tally.

#### Overview

* Suvit supports up to 4 decimal points. However, the number of decimal points used depends on the Tally settings.
* For example, if Tally is set to 3 decimal points, Suvit will also follow the same.

### You can change the necessary settings in Tally Prime

#### Step 1

![1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_c8bbcb3c61.png)

* Go to → **Company** → **Alter**

#### Step 2

![2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_3adb7252c9.png)

* Press **F12** (Configure) → provide additional base currency: **Yes** → press **Ctrl+A** to save.

#### Step 3

![3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_994d6584e6.png)

* Change the required **Decimal point**

#### Step 4

![4.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_493e186f74.png)

* **Open Suvit Desktop Application** → **Refresh or Sync voucher** to get synced with Suvit
* Then Refresh your Website Page.
* You have now successfully completed the process.
