# What is Document Management in Suvit ?

Manage client-wise documents on Suvit’s cloud platform, accessible from anywhere. You can also store additional documents beyond those uploaded in Suvit.

---

### Document Management

* **“Document Management”** helps you upload and organize client documents easily. You can see all your clients' folders in one place, making it faster to upload and manage documents.

#### Step 1: Sign In

![1Document1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_Document1_c28c5b629e.png)

* [Sign In](https://www.suvit.io/) to Suvit.
* On the left menu, find **Document**.

#### Step 2: Open Documents

![2Doc1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_Doc1_5de483239c.png)

* Click on **Documents** to open the Document Management section. Here, you can see company-wise data, search for a company folder, and access module folders.

#### Step 3: Upload & Organize

![4doc3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4doc3_df1d1815e6.png)

* You will find folders for different modules, like shown in the image above.
* You can upload documents into these **folders,** and even store personal documents in the **Other** folder. You can access and download these documents anytime.
