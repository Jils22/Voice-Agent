# How to reset your Suvit account password?

Forgot your Suvit password? Don’t worry! You can easily reset it in just a few simple steps and regain access to your account quickly and securely.

---

### Reset Your Suvit Account Password: A Step-by-Step Guide

#### Step 1: Visit the Suvit Website

![24Forgot pwd1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/24_Forgot_pwd1_32e49a7c54.png)

* Go to [Suvit.io](https://www.suvit.io/)
* Click on **Forgot Password** or use this direct link. [Click here](https://in.suvit.io/forgot-password)

#### Step 2: Enter Registered Details

![25forgot pwd2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/25forgot_pwd2_d680feede6.png)

* After selecting **Forgot Password**, you'll be redirected to the reset page.
* Enter your **registered email ID or mobile number** and click **Get OTP**.

#### Step 3: Enter OTP

![26forgot pwd3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/26forgot_pwd3_36d41a09be.png)

* An OTP will be sent to your registered **email or mobile number**.
* Enter the OTP and click **Submit**.

#### Step 4: Set New Password

![4 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_img_4869c54517.png)

* After OTP verification, you’ll be redirected to the **New Password** page.
* Enter and confirm your new password, then click **Submit** to complete the reset.
