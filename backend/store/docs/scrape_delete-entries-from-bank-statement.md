# Delete entries from bank statement

We will guide you to ignore or delete the not required entries from a bank statement uploaded in Suvit for transferring it to tally.

---

### Delete entries from bank statement

The bank statement you uploaded in **Suvit** might contain some entries that are already recorded in **Tally** or entries that are **contra** in nature and posted from another bank statement.  
To avoid duplication, **Suvit** provides an option to delete such entries once identified.

#### Step 1: Filter Entries

* Use the **filters** available in Suvit to identify the entries that you wish to delete.

#### Step 2: Select Entries

* Select the entries you want to delete. The **Delete** button will appear once the entries are selected, as shown below:  
  ![1Delete Entry.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_Delete_Entry_dea7a88e15.png)

#### Step 3: Delete Entries

* Click on the **Delete** button to remove the selected entries from the bank statement in Suvit.  
  ![2Delete entry 2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_Delete_entry_2_5c1bd5232f.png)

#### Important Notes:

* The **Delete** option will only be visible after you select the entries.
* Once deleted, the entries cannot be recovered.
* These entries will be deleted **from Suvit** but not from the **original bank statement**.
