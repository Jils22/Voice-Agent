# Suvit account is deactivated.

Your Suvit account will be deactivated if you consecutively enter wrong password for 5 times, follow this article steps to re-activate your account.

#### Forgot Password

* If user entered the wrong password 5 consecutive times, your account will be deactivated.

![1 forget img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_forget_img_d36d9097f7.png)

* To **activate** your account use [Forgot Password](https://in.suvit.io/forgot-password) option . For more details, [click here](https://help.suvit.io/articles/how-to-reset-your-suvit-account-password) for entire step.

#### Secondary Account

##### There are two methods to activate Secondary account

**Method : 1** → Use [Forgot Password](https://in.suvit.io/forgot-password) option.

**Method : 2** → This step is performed by **Primary User** only
![2 img user.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_img_user_557a0739b6.png)

* After login - under **Home**
* Go to **"User & Roles"**
* Click on **De-Active** in front of the user account

![3 active img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_active_img_bb7b0f5b9e.png)

* Click on the **"Active"** button, and your secondary account will be successfully activated.
