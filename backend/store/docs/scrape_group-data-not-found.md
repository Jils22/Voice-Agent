# Handling Missing Group Data | Suvit Product Help

If a group is missing in your Tally data, it may be due to deletion or changes. This guide helps you identify the issue and provides steps to resolve it.

### ❓ Group Data Not Found Error in Suvit

If you see a **"Group Data Not Found"** message while **syncing data in Suvit**, it means Suvit is trying to access a group that **does not exist** in your Tally company data. This issue may occur due to:

* The group being **Deleted** or has been **Modified** in Tally

---

### 🛠️ **How to Fix This Issue?**

**Steps:**

1. **Do not modify the original groups** in Tally.
2. **Check if all groups are present** in Tally.

* **Create a new group** manually in Tally.
* **Sync Master** data from the **Suvit Desktop Application**.

3. **Try syncing again** after ensuring all required groups exist in Tally.

---

#### Create a New Group in Tally

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Gateway of Tally** → **Create** → **Groups** (Under Accounting Master).
2. **Sync Master** data from the **Suvit Desktop Application**

#### **How to Modify an Existing Group in Tally?**

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Gateway of Tally** → **Alter** → **Groups** (Under Accounting Master).
2. **Sync Master** data from the **Suvit Desktop Application**

---

#### Check extra space for in Company Name

**Step-by-Step Guide:**

* From the Gateway of Tally, press the Alt+K (Company) key.
* In the Company menu, select Alter.
* From the List of Companies that appears, select the company you wish to alter.
* The Company Alteration screen will open.
* Go the end of the company name and remove extra space.
  Press Ctrl+A to accept and save the changes.

If the issue still persists while syncing, please reach out to **Suvit Support** for further assistance. 🚀
