# How to Register in Suvit

Simplify your accounting with Suvit! Create your account today and unlock powerful automation. Follow these easy steps to get started in no time.

### Welcome to Suvit

#### Step 1: For Registration

![1 website.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_website_f2fb1a5dd7.png)

* Visit our [Suvit Website](https://in.suvit.io/signUp)

#### Step 2: Enter Mobile Number

![2 signup.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_signup_a7dc8c0cc7.png)

* Enter your **mobile number.**
* Click on **GET OTP**.(You will receive OTP on your number.)

![2.1 enter otp.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_1_enter_otp_5df240ef08.png)

* Enter **OTP**.
* Then click on **Sign In**.

#### Step 3: Fill Personal Information

* Fill Other information to create your Profile
  ![3 Personal Information.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_Personal_Information_da9cf6f264.png)
* Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
* Select your **Profession**.
* Then click on **Next**.

#### Step 4: Organization Information

![4 Organization Information.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_Organization_Information_ef424dcfaa.png)

* Enter **Number of Employees or Staff**.
* **How did you find out about us?**.
* Then click on **Next**.

#### Step 5: Set Your Password

![5. Set your password.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/5_Set_your_password_fac64c018f.png)

* Enter your **E-Mail ID**.
* Set your **Password**. (Minimum 8 Characters)
* Then click on **Next**.

#### Step 6: Welcome to Suvit

* Your Registration has been done successfully.
