# Unable to see Bank name ledger name while importing bank statement ?

If the bank ledger name is missing from the dropdown while uploading a bank statement, your ledgers may not have synced yet.

---

#### Steps to sync ledger:

#### Step 1: Open Suvit.io in Browser

![1dashboard.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1dashboard_f7f33bb8b1.png)

* Sign in into [Suvit](https://www.suvit.io/) account.

#### Step 2: Open Tally and Select the Company

![2image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2image_6138920928.png)

* Launch **Tally** on your system and\*\* select\*\* the **company** you want to sync.

#### Step 3: Use Suvit Desktop Application

![3sync master.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3sync_master_0942e74a56.png)

* Open the **Suvit Desktop Application.**
* Navigate to **My Companies** and find the company where "Sync Master" is available.
* Click on **Sync Master.**

#### Step 4: Verify Successful Sync

![4sync master1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4sync_master1_295995f1d8.png)

* Once you see a popup confirming that the **ledger synced successfully**, go to **Suvit Web Login**.
* Refresh the page using **CTRL+SHIFT+R.**
