# Save entries and send transactions to Tally

After successfully uploading of bank statement, you need to select & save the ledgers for each transaction & send those entries into Tally.

---

### Save Entries and Send Transactions to Tally

#### Step 1: Open the Completed Bank Statement

![2sent to tally.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2sent_to_tally_0e1249a350.png)

* Once your bank statement is marked as [**Complete**](https://help.suvit.io/articles/complete-status-after-processing-the-statement),click to open it.
* Select ledgers for each transaction. You can also use bulk ledger selection for similar entries that need to be posted in a common ledger.

#### Step 2: Save Ledgers and Send to Tally

![1send to tally.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1send_to_tally_380106ae18.png)

* After **Selecting all ledgers**, click the **Save** button to apply the ledger selections.
* Click the **Send To Tally** button to begin the syncing process.

#### Step 3: Track Progress

##### **A → Gray Stage (Initiation)**

![1_1_img_5eeaf4fc0e.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_1_img_5eeaf4fc0e_22670acd70.png)

* Indicates that the process has been initiated.

##### **B → Orange Stage (In Progress)**

![1_2_img_19cdd31ea7.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_2_img_19cdd31ea7_4a299798fd.png)

* Indicates that the data transfer process has started.

##### **C → Green Stage (Success)**

![1_3_img_1b4b7effe5.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_3_img_1b4b7effe5_b010a7f043.png)

* Indicates that the purchase data has been successfully sent to Tally.
* If the transaction fails to sync with Tally, refer to [this article](https://help.suvit.io/articles/transaction-are-getting-fail-while-pushing-data-from-suvit-to-tally) to troubleshoot and re-sync the data.
