# Create bulk ledger in Suvit

Suvit helps in creating 1000s of ledgers in bulk within some minutes. Here you can also utilize the GST Mapping feature to create ledgers using GST Portal

### **Create Bulk Ledgers in Suvit**

### How to do Bulk Ledger Creation in Suvit?

Bulk ledger creation allows you to upload multiple ledgers at once using a simple Excel format. Follow the steps below to get started:

#### **Step 1: Login to Suvit**

Go to <https://in.suvit.io> and log in with your registered credentials.

![1 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_img_53a3d02d3f.png)

---

#### **Step 2: Go to Bulk Upload Section**

![2 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_img_fceb8458f4.png)

* From the left sidebar, click on the **Bulk Upload** icon under **Data Entry Automation**
* Select **Ledger** under **Bulk Upload**

---

#### \*\*Step 3: Sample Sheet

![1bulkledger.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1bulkledger_2040982dc5.png)

* One can use our sample sheet or else can upload there own excel sheet. Method is same for both type.

---

#### **Step 4: Fill the Sample Excel**

![12022-11-16_1035512016.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/12022_11_16_1035512016_5811a5bc1b.png)

Open the downloaded file and fill in the ledger details.

**Important Notes:**

* Properly mention the under type of the party name

---

#### Step 5: Upload Excel sheet

![3bulkledger1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3bulkledger1_9e1094ac1e.png)

* On the top right, click the **Upload** icon to upload the file

---

#### **Step 6: Upload the Filled Excel File**

![5.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/5_8977ab0941.webp)

* Click on **Upload**.
* Drag and drop your file or click to select.
* Finally, press the **Upload** button to complete the process.

---

#### **Step 7: Verify the Upload**

![6 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/6_img_39ddf8ee53.png)

* Once uploaded, you can view your file in the ledger list **Click** on the file to open.

---

#### Step 8: Map Fields for Ledgers

#### **Method 1: GST Mapping for Party Creation**

![GST MAP 1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/GST_MAP_1_cfbaf97903.png)

* Select the **GST Field** in the uploaded Excel sheet.
* Click **GST MAPPING** to fetch details from the GST Portal.

![GST MAP 2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/GST_MAP_2_6b6ff8ed55.png)

* The system retrieves **Party Name, State, and Tax Information** based on the GST Number.
* Choose the name as per **"Trade Name"** or **"Business Name"** from the Which name would you prefer to use for ledger creation? as shown above image.
* Proceed to the next step for **Save & Proceed** .

#### **Method 2: Map Fields for Party Creation**

![ledger map 1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/ledger_map_1_3377514d31.png)

* After uploading, you will be taken to the **Mapping Field Page**. Map the **Excel sheet fields** with the system fields and click **"Save & Proceed"** to finalize the **ledger creation.**

#### Step 9: Confirmation

![6master6.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/6master6_3e19e5a385.png)

* If there are any issues, a **popup** will display the errors.
* You can view the **errors** directly or download the **Excel error log** for reference.

#### Step 7: Save & Send

![8master7.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/8master7_2f895b76a2.png)

* Go back to the **Ledger Transaction Page**, select the record you want to import, and click **Save**.
* Click on the right-side option **"Send to Tally"**.

#### Step 10: Send to Tally

![10master9.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/10master9_e9ca146d58.png)

* A confirmation **popup** will appear. Click **OK** to proceed or **Cancel** to stop.

![11master10.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/11master10_32cd4cdab3.png)

* The system will **sync data with Tally**, ensuring successful synchronization.

#### Step 11: Verify in Tally

* After receiving the message **"Your data process has been completed"**, check Tally.
* The **ledger masters** will now be available in **Tally**.
