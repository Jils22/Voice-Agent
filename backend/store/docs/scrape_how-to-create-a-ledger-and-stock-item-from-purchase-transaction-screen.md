# Create a ledger and stock item from purchase transaction screen

If ledgers are missing on the Purchase screen, use the “Create Ledger” option to add them instantly. Follow the steps in this article to proceed smoothly.

#### In Suvit, you can create:

* **Ledgers** → For Party Names
* **Stock Items** → For Goods you sell or buy

There are two simple ways to do it:
👉 One-by-One (Single Entry)
👉 All-at-Once (Bulk Entry)

---

### ✅ Method A: Create One-by-One (Single)

---

### 👤 1. Create a Party Name (Ledger)

#### Step 1: Click to Create

![1 IMG.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_IMG_33f46da6b0.png)

* Click the **Create Ledger** button

---

#### Step 2: Fill Party Details

![2 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_img_e23a165bac.png)

* A new pop-up will open
  → Add party name, GST number, state, etc.

---

#### Step 3: Save the Ledger

![3 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_img_7b02ea8258.png)

* Click **Add** to save the party account

```
💡 Tip: Once added, send one transaction to Tally — Suvit will create the ledger and then push the entry.
```

---

### 📦 2. Create a Stock Item (Only for Item Invoices)

#### Step 1: Open the Item Menu

![4 item img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_item_img_89bcf1bb3a.png)

* Click on the **drawer icon** to open stock settings

---

#### Step 2: Click “Create Item”

![5 item img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/5_item_img_93290ef992.png)

* Select **Create Item**

---

#### Step 3: Fill Item Details & Save

![6 item img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/6_item_img_d95cefc74f.png)

* Add name, unit, GST %, etc.
* Click **Add** to save the item

```
💡 Tip: Send one invoice to Tally — the item gets created first, then the data is pushed.
```

---

## 📥 Method B: Bulk Creation (Multiple Entries)

---

### 👥 1. Bulk Create Party Names

![3.1 img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_1_img_f2fbffb556.png)

* Click the **orange Plus Icon** to add many party names together
* For full instructions, [click here](https://help.suvit.io/articles/bulk-party-creation-sales-purchase)

```
📌 Note: Once you send data to Tally, the ledger is auto-created there.
```

---

### 📦 2. Bulk Create Stock Items

![6.1 bulk img.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/6_1_bulk_img_6a8a1db23d.png)

* Click the **orange Plus Icon** to add many stock items at once

```
📌 Note: New stock items will be created in Tally as soon as you push the data.
```
