# Uploading Journal data through an excel sheet

This FAQ will guide you through the simple process of uploading your Journal data using Excel in Suvit. Follow the visual steps below.

### 📌 Follow the Steps Below:

#### Step 1: Go to Bulk Upload > Journal

![1.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_1d40fe20b6.webp)

1. Click on **Bulk Upload** in the left-side menu.
2. In the dropdown, choose **Journal** from the list of document types.

---

#### Step 2: Upload Your Excel File

![2.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_28d5ce7688.webp)

3. Click on the **Upload** button in the top-right.

---

![3.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_61bcde8615.webp)

4. Drag and drop your Excel file **or** click the upload box to browse and select your file manually.
5. Then click the **Upload** button at the bottom to begin the upload.

---

#### Step 3: View the Uploaded Journal File

![4.webp](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_867b3e7a6c.webp)

6. Your uploaded file will now appear in the list. You will see:

→ **File Name**

→ **Invoice Type** (e.g., Accounting Invoice)

→ **Total Records**

→ **Pending Records**

This confirms the sheet has been uploaded and is ready for mapping.
