# How to Subscribe split Company in Suvit

After splitting a subscribed company in Suvit, you can use the split version in place of the original without using any additional credits.

---

```
**How to split Company in Tally follow the link from here**
```

```
**Note* : If you create New Company with same details it will not considered as Split company**
```

```
Before split any company make sure all work is done.
```

If you split the company from Tally, Suvit allows you to subscribe split company without using extra credit, but if you like to work on both Company (Main Company & Split Company), then 1 credit will be utilized. Also, further if you again split the company (Making 2nd split), respective credit will be utilized.

### Steps to subscribe the Split Company in Suvit

![1Open20Company.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_Open20_Company_154b8c2c55.jpg)

#### Step 1 - Open Split Company in Tally

1. Launch Tally.
2. Open the company that was split.

![2Open20Application(copy).png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_Open20_Application_copy_7499173948.png)

#### Step 2 - Open Suvit Application

1. Login into [Suvit](https://in.suvit.io/signIn)

```
**Note: Previous company will show disconnected in Suvit Connecter App.**
```

#### Step 3 - Search Your Split Company

1. Go to **Company** in the menu.
2. Search for your split company name.

![3split.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3split_f51e59db59.png)

#### Step 4 - Enable the Company

1. Company status will show as **Disabled**.
2. Click on **Disable** to activate (enable) the split company.
3. Both the main company and the split company will be displayed as **disabled**.
4. If the company is not displayed, kindly refresh the page.

![4split1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4split1_2de1154397.png)

#### Step 5 - Confirm Subscription

1. Click **OK to continue**.

![5split%20confirmation.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/5split_20confirmation_41a582d76f.jpg)

### Final Step - Sync the Company

1. Sync the split company from Suvit Application.
2. Your split company is now ready to use in Suvit without extra credit consumption.

```
**Note*:- Once Split company is subscribed - credit cannot be reversed or exchange or interchange in any condition.**
```
