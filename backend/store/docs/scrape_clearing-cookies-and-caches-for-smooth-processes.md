# Clearing cookies and caches for smooth processes.

This error you may face when you are trying to push data from Suvit to tally to solve clear Browser cache.

---

## Clearing Cookies and Cache for Smooth Processes

### How to Clean Cache & Cookies?

### Step 1

* Right-click on the Transaction screen where you are facing the issue and click on **Inspect**.

![1!image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_image_9522987366.png)

### Step 2

* After clicking on **Inspect**, a small window will appear on the right side of the screen.

![2@image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_image_c28784b462.png)

### Step 3

* In the small window, click on the small arrow **>>**, then select **Application**.

![3#image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_image_2c2ac82852.png)

### Step 4

* Go to **Storage**, then click on **Including third-party cookies**, and finally click on **Clear site data**.

![4$image.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/4_image_5b87cb6fbc.png)

After this, refresh the page, sign in again, and try to push the data. The issue should be resolved.
