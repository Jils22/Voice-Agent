# How to Select or Change the Company in Suvit

Learn how to select or switch companies in Suvit. Follow step-by-step instructions and get tips to fix missing companies or switching errors with ease.

### How to Select or Change the Company in Suvit

#### Overview:

* In Suvit, you can manage multiple businesses by switching between company accounts. Follow the steps below to change or select a company.

---

### Step-by-Step Guide

#### 🧭 Step 1: Go to Dashboard

![1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_4d43bd45f9.png)

→ Once you log in to Suvit, click on the **Dashboard** option from the left-hand menu.
→ This is your central hub to view company activity, transactions, and top customers.
→ Select Company from the **Right Side Top Corner**.

#### 🔍 Step 2: Open Company Switcher

![2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_27089390cb.png)

→ Click on the company name or dropdown at the top-right corner of your dashboard.
→ A side panel titled “My Company” will appear.
→ Use the **search bar** (highlighted in red) to find a specific company.
→ Click the **switch icon** (highlighted in green) beside the company you want to switch to.

#### ✅ Step 3: Confirm the Company Switch

![3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_dbbec404db.png)

→ A confirmation pop-up will appear asking if you're sure you want to switch companies.
→ Click **Yes** to proceed.

---

### Troubleshooting Tips:

#### **Issue 1: Missing Company in the Dropdown**

**Cause:** The company might not be added to your account.

**Solution:**

* Check if the company is added, if not [add company](https://help.suvit.io/articles/Subscribe-company-and-sync)
* Check with the admin to confirm that the company is associated with your account.
* If you’re an admin, verify that the company details are added in the **User Management Module**.

#### **Issue 2: Unable to Switch Companies**

**Cause:** A technical glitch or restricted permissions might be the issue.
**Solution:**

* Refresh the page
* or Log out and log back in to refresh the session.
* Clear your browser cache and try again.
* Contact support if the issue persists.
