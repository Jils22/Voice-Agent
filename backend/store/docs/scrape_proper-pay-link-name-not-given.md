### Common Error: Proper Pay Link Name not given for

#### Possible Cause

* This error appears while splitting the data in **Tally.ERP 9 Release 4.8**, if the data was migrated from Release 4.7. This may be due to incomplete migration.

### Solution:

#### Step 1

* Take Backup of your Tally Data

#### Step 2

* Update your **Tally Erp 9** to latest Version
* Download the latest version of Tally ERP 9 from [Tally's official website.](https://tallysolutions.com/download/support-files/)

**OR**

* Connect your Tally Vendor or Tally customer care for update
