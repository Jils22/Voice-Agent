## Suggested Ledgers

#### Suvit Creates Auto Suggestions for You to Work Easily

* Suvit allows you to select ledgers in bulk by typing keywords in the description section. With the help of AI, Suvit stores that pattern, and next time when such narrations are identified by Suvit in the same statement for the next month, it will give you suggestions.
* Refer to the below image for a better understanding:

![1suggestedledger.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1suggestedledger_83c7dc48f5.png)

* Simply, click on the **funnel (Filter)** button, and click on the box of **"Fill"** as shown in the below image. This will filter all the suggested transactions:

![2suggestedledger1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2suggestedledger1_a608743a4f.png)

* There is a small arrow button beside **"Suggested Ledgers"**. Click on it, and all the ledgers will be assigned to transactions at once, reducing your time by up to 90%:

![3suggestedledger2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3suggestedledger2_be25832b91.png)
