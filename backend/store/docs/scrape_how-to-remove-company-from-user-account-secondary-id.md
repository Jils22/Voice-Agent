# How to remove company from user account (secondary id)

Learn how to remove a company assigned to a user in Suvit. Follow this step-by-step guide to manage user access and keep your company list organized.

### How to Remove a Company from a Secondary Account

#### Step 1: Sign In

![1signin.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1signin_ee7d1e2855.png)

* Login to your **primary account** by clicking [Sign in](https://in.suvit.io/signUp).

#### Step 2: Access User Management

![2usermgt2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2usermgt2_6a1758f95d.png)

* Navigate to **User Management** to view the list of users.
* On the right-hand side, locate the **eye icon** under the **Actions** column and click on it.

#### Step 3: Remove the Company

![3usermgt3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3usermgt3_a37d31ca0c.png)

* In the list, under **Actions**, click the **delete icon** next to the company you want to remove.
* After refreshing, the company will be removed but retained in the **primary account**.
