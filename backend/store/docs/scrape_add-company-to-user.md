# How to add company to the user?

Grant specific company access to a user in Suvit, allowing them to view and manage relevant data. Ideal for controlling permissions based on user roles.

### **How to Assign a Company to a User?**

#### Step 1. **Access the User Section:**

* Log in to your **Primary (Main) Account**.
* Navigate to the **User & Roles** section.

#### Step 2. **Assign the Company:**

![1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_cadf638405.png)

* Click on **"Assign Company"**.

![2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_539a0bb794.png)

* A drop-down menu will appear.
* Search and select the **required company** from the list.

#### Step 3. **Finalize the Assignment:**

![3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_30685f3f83.png)

* Click on **"Assign Company"** to give the selected user access to the chosen company.

### **Important Notes:**

* **Full Access by Default:**  
  If the user has been assigned **full access by default**, they will automatically have access to **all companies** without the need to assign them individually.
