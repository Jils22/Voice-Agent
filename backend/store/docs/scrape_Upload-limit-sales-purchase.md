# You cannot upload data more than 10000!

Ensure your Excel sheet in Suvit has fewer than 10000 rows. If you face errors while uploading, refer to the article below for successful data upload steps.

### You Cannot Upload Data More Than 7000!

When you are uploading an Excel sheet with data exceeding 7000 rows, the file will not be uploaded due to a restriction on the size of data per Excel sheet. This is because when transferring such large data, Tally becomes unresponsive, which may further lead to errors.

If you have more than 7000 rows of data, simply split it into different Excel sheets and then upload them. Suvit has no restrictions on the number of Excel sheets you can import.

### Troubleshooting for Less Than 7000 Data:

Sometimes, even when the data is less than 7000, you may encounter this issue. This can be due to the Excel sheet format. To resolve it:

1. Select the entire blank rows and blank columns in the Excel sheet.
2. Delete them using **CTRL -** (Control & Minus sign).
3. Save the sheet and re-upload it. The issue should be resolved.
