# Sorry we are facing some issue at our end

Windows Firewall, Security, or third-party antivirus may block Suvit. Learn how to unblock the app in your system settings for smooth operation.

## Sorry We Are Facing Some Issues on Our End

When this type of error occurs during login to the **Suvit** application:

![sorry_error.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/sorry_error_7bca98d91e.png)

* Sometimes, **Windows Firewall** or **Windows Security** and any installed third-party antivirus block the Suvit application. Unblock the app in your security or firewall networks.
* If you have installed third-party antivirus (e.g., Quick Heal Antivirus, Kaspersky Antivirus, NPAV Total Security, etc.), you need to add the Suvit application to the exclusive rules in the antivirus settings.

### Kindly Follow These Steps:

#### 1. Allow Suvit Through Windows Firewall

* Open the Windows Search bar, type **Windows Firewall**, and select **Allow an app through Windows Firewall**.

![Step_1&2.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_1_and_2_df2abda2e9.jpg)

* Click **Change settings**.

![Step_03.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_03_e9858d4733.jpg)

* Click **Allow another app**.

![Step_04.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_04_11d2507cc2.jpg)

* Click **Browse** and navigate to the desktop.

![Step_05.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_05_384f50960c.jpg)

* Select the Suvit shortcut and click **Open**.

![Step_06-07.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_06_07_16ac4b0599.jpg)

* Click **Add**.

![Step_08.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_08_0cdcf52c4a.jpg)

* Ensure Suvit is selected, then check both **Private** and **Public** options for Suvit. Click **OK** to save your changes.

![Step_09.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_09_5802ccf256.jpg)

#### 2. Disable Antivirus Real-Time Protection and Add Exclusions

* Open the Windows Search bar, type **Virus and threat protection**, and select it.

![Step_10V1.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_10_V1_92d1939884.jpg)

* Click **Manage settings**.

![Step_10V2.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_10_V2_318fa89cbc.jpg)

* Turn off the **Real-time protection** toggle.

![Step_11V.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_11_V_22b7c105f1.jpg)

* Scroll down and find **Exclusions**, then click **Add or remove exclusions**.

![Step_12V.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_12_V_22c3fda69a.jpg)

#### 3. Add Folder and File Exclusions

* Click **Add an exclusion**, then select **Folder**.

![Step_13V.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_13_V_bb787edcf2.jpg)

* Press **Windows + R** to open the Run dialog. Type **appdata** and click **OK**.

![Step_14V.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_14_V_7cfc2a5fde.jpg)

* In the AppData folder, navigate to **Roaming > Suvit > Database**. Select this folder and click **Select Folder**.

![Step_15V.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_15_V_554deae897.jpg)

* Repeat the exclusion process but choose **File** this time.

![Step_16V.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_16_V_be39c6b441.jpg)

* Press **Windows + R** again, type **appdata**, and click **OK**. Navigate to **Local > Program > Suvit** and select the relevant files.

![Step_17V.jpg](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/Step_17_V_d2ccd4ddce.jpg)

#### 4. Restart Your PC

* After completing the steps above, restart your PC and try login
