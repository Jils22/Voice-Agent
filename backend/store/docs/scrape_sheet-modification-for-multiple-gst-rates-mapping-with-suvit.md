# Sheet modification for Multiple GST rates & Mapping with Suvit

Learn to prepare Excel data with multiple GST rates for processing in multiple GST ledgers in Tally. This guide also covers proper mapping steps in Suvit.

---

### How to Enter GST Data in Tally

* If you want to enter data in Tally for different tax types like **SGST, CGST, or IGST** (for example, **SGST 9%, CGST 9%, SGST 14%, CGST 14%** ), follow these easy steps:

#### Step 1: Prepare Your Excel Sheet

* Make a new column with the title **Sales or Purchase Ledger**. This will be where you write what the data is for (like "Sales").
* Create columns for **SGST**, **CGST**, and **IGST**.

#### Step 2: Apply Filter

* Use the filter option in Excel to sort the data by **Rate** (like 18%).

#### Step 3: Add Data to the Columns

![Filter Example](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2filter_eaf6aaa794.jpg)

* After filtering the rate (for example, 18%), put the names of the Sales/Purchase ledger, **SGST, CGST, and IGST** in the right columns based on your Tally names.
* Your data should look like above image

#### Step 4: Upload and Map Your Data

* After you finish preparing your data in Excel, upload the document to Suvit.
* Click on the **Complete** button to map your data correctly.

#### Step 5: Select GST Ledgers

![GST Mapping](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3gst_mapping4_6053e529c8.png)

* When asked about the GST Ledger, choose **Yes** for each column.
* A new section will open where you can check the boxes for the correct fields.

#### Step 6: Save and Proceed

* After mapping, click on **Save & Proceed** to move your data to the next step.
