# How to Integrate Vyapar with Suvit in Four Easy Steps

Integrate Vyapar with Suvit in 4 simple steps—enable sync, link accounts, grant access & sync data. Follow the step-by-step guide with images for setup.

---

### Follow the four easy steps

#### Step 1 - Enable Sync in Vyapar

**1**. Open **Vyapar** and select the **company** you want to integrate.

**2**. Navigate to **Sync, Share, and Backup**.

**3**. Enable **Sync** to allow data sharing with Suvit.

---

#### Step 2 - Link Vyapar with Suvit

**1**. Login into [Suvit](https://in.suvit.io/signIn). Go to **Company**

**2**. Vyapar integration

![1.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/1_8ffd2d64cf.png)

* Under Vyapar Integration click on **Connect Now** in front of the desired company

**3**. Enter details

![2.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/2_7fe2082621.png)

* Enter details related to **Vyapar Company** → click on **Check**

**4**. Send OTP

![3.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/3_69abbb6dd4.png)

* Click on **Send OTP** → enter **Vyapar registered number** → **Send OTP**

---

#### Step 3 - Grant Account Access in Vyapar

**1**. Go to **Vyapar → Utilities → Account Access**.

**2**. Click on **Retry**.

**3**. Enter the **OTP** received and confirm the access. (*OTP will be received on WhatsApp*)

![12.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/12_0c2d7216f7.png)

---

#### Step 4 - Sync Data in Suvit

**1**. In **Suvit**, navigate to **Data Entry Automation → Explore Now → Master → Mapping**.

![17.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/17_cb3ce6746c.png)

**2**. Click on **Sync**.

![18.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/18_7f4ab4d7f3.png)

* Wait for a few minutes (depending on the company data size).

**3**. Once the sync is complete, your Vyapar data will be available in Suvit.

![19.png](https://strapi.suvit.io/uploads/19_2b391815d9.png)

---

### 📝 Notes:

* Ensure that the company in **Vyapar** matches the one selected in **Suvit**.
* OTP will be sent via **email or WhatsApp**, depending on your Vyapar login method.
* The initial sync may take **several minutes** if the company has a large amount of data.
